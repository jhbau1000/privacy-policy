Privacy Policy

This privacy policy outlines how Baulance (the "Application"), developed by James Bau, handles user and device data.

1. Data Collection and Developer Access

Baulance does not collect, store, or monitor any financial or personal data. The developer, James Bau, has zero access to your checkbook registers, transactions, or personal details. All financial logging stays entirely local to your own device.

2. QR Code Peer-to-Peer Sharing

The Application features a tool allowing users to share register info with family members. This transfer happens strictly phone-to-phone via a locally generated QR code. The data embedded in the QR code is fully encrypted during transmission. No data passes through a developer server during this transaction.

3. Age Restrictions and Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for, marketed to, or designed for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly target or collect data from children under 13.

4. Changes to This Policy

This policy may change over time. Any updates will be pushed directly to this public webpage.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact James Bau through the official app store developer channel.
